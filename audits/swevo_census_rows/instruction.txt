LAYOUT RULE FOR THIS CORPUS. Many tables here print the mean and the standard deviation of an optimizer as SEPARATE ROWS of the same table (a row labelled Mean, Ave, Avg or Average and, directly beneath it, a row labelled Std, SD, S.D. or Standard deviation, for the same function) or as SEPARATE COLUMNS (a Mean column and a Std column per optimizer). Treat such a Std cell as the dispersion of the mean it belongs to: set any_error_bar to y, bar_form to separate_row or separate_column, and for every single-bold group give winner_disp and runner_disp from the Std cells that belong to the bold mean and to the best non-bold mean. Groups are the rows or columns of MEANS only: never list a Std, SD, Rank, Best, Worst, Median or Time row as a group of its own, and never record a Std cell as a winner_mean or runner_mean.
