\section{Contents of the supplementary archive}
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{study/}: the instrument at its frozen commit (parser, comparison
  rules, tests, reference-set tooling), unchanged from the tag it was frozen at.
\item \texttt{aamas/}: the AAMAS-specific scripts: the proceedings index, the
  analysis driver, the protocol scan, the community classifier, the macro
  generator, the figure.
\item \texttt{data/extracted\_aamas.jsonl}: every table cell the parser read
  from the \nAPapers{} papers, with position, bold flag, mean and bar.
\item \texttt{out/aamas/}: every run record a number in the paper comes from,
  including the per-comparison database (\texttt{analysis.json}), the
  community labels, the protocol sentences, and the reference-set scores.
\item \texttt{audits/aamas\_gold/}: the \nGTestableN{} testable and
  \nGNoBarN{} bar-less reference items, rendered, with the reader's labels.
\item \texttt{audits/aamas\_gold\_gemini/}: the second reader's labels on the
  same reference items.
\item \texttt{audits/aamas\_pages/}: the \nPagesRead{} random pages of the
  pilot with the first reader's end-to-end labels.
\item \texttt{audits/aamas\_census/}: the \nCzPagesDrawn{} caption pages of
  the census with the second reader's labels (\nCzPagesRead{} read).
\item \texttt{audits/aamas\_testable\_pages/}: the \nTpPages{} pages holding
  parser-testable comparisons, read a second time for the test--retest check.
\item \texttt{run\_all\_aamas.sh}: regenerates every number and the macros.
\end{itemize}

\section{The census protocol}
\label{app:census}
Every page on which the parser detected a table caption (\nCzPagesDrawn{}
pages from \nCzPapersDrawn{} papers) was rendered at twice its printed size
and sent, with the paper's run-count sentences, to the second reader (Gemini
3.1 Pro, temperature 0) under the instructions below, emitted verbatim from
\texttt{study/read\_pages\_api.py} by the build. The protocol was fixed
before the reading began (\texttt{git tag census-protocol-v1}). Pages whose
answer was not valid JSON after three attempts were retried in a separate
run. Fifteen pages, among the densest in the corpus, timed out at the relay
on every attempt at the model's default reasoning setting (one of them
carries a hundred single-bold groups over three tables); they were read
with the reasoning effort set to \texttt{low}, everything else identical.
To see what that setting changes, \nRePages{} pages the census had read at
the default setting, drawn at random among those listing a group, were read
again at \texttt{low}: the second read matches \nReMatchedCi{} of the first
read's \nReGroupsFirst{} groups and lists \nReGroupsSecond{}; the bar-less
verdict agrees on \nReNeitherAgreePct{} of the matched groups, testability on
\nReTestableAgreePct{}, and the Welch verdict on \nReFailAgree{} of the
\nReBothTested{} both could test; bar-less shares \nReNeitherFirst{} against
\nReNeitherSecond{}, failure rates \nReFailFirst{} against \nReFailSecond{}
(\texttt{out/aamas/reader\_effort.json}). \nCzPagesRead{} of the
\nCzPagesDrawn{} pages were read. The reading is
scored by \texttt{study/pages\_score.py}, the same script that scores the
pilot, matching each reader group to at most one parser comparison on the
winner's printed value.

The \nTpPages{} pages holding parser-testable comparisons had been read by
the same model in an earlier run under the same instructions; on the
\nRtPages{} of them the census read, the second
read matches \nRtMatchedCi{} of the first read's \nRtGroupsFirst{} groups
(\nRtMatchedOfSecondPct{} of the second read's \nRtGroupsSecond{}), the
bar-less verdict agrees on \nRtNeitherAgree{} of the \nRtMatched{} matched
groups (\nRtNeitherAgreePct{}), testability on \nRtTestableAgreePct{}, and
the Welch verdict on \nRtFailAgree{} of the \nRtBothTested{} groups both
reads could test; the failure rates of the two reads are \nRtFailFirst{}
and \nRtFailSecond{} (\texttt{aamas/reader\_retest.py}).

On the \nCcrPages{} random pilot pages both readers read, the first lists
\nCcrFirstGroups{} groups and the second \nCcrSecondGroups{}, \nCcrMatched{}
of which match (\nCcrMatchedPct{} of the first's); their bar-less shares are
\nCcrFirstNeither{} and \nCcrSecondNeither{}.

\section{Evaluation-protocol patterns}
\label{app:patterns}
The sentences that state an evaluation episode count or a seed count were
found with the regular expressions in \texttt{aamas/protocol.py}; every match
is in \texttt{out/aamas/protocol.json} with its sentence. A count is the
smallest stated in the paper; a paper stating none is counted as not stating
one, which under-counts protocols described in words the patterns miss.
